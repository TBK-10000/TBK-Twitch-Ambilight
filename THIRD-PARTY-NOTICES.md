are Apache-2.0 licensed. See the [upstream license](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright/blob/main/LICENSE).
The source project includes a dependency declaration and TBK test scripts.
The manual installation ZIP excludes developer files, Playwright, its transitive
dependencies and browser binaries. They are not loaded by the extension.
Future redistribution of those packages
requires retaining their own license/notice files. No runtime npm dependency is bundled.

## TBK artwork

The vector screen/mustache icon in `icons/tbk.svg`, its PNG renders and the inline
settings gear were drawn with geometric SVG primitives specifically for TBK.
No upstream logo, marketing image or icon library is used. They are covered by
the root MIT license. The grain texture is generated by `src/chat.js`; fonts are
system fonts, with no font files distributed.
